    How this works

    Press HO HO HO and you get one card. Read it out loud and play it. Points are on the card. Not feeling it? Skip it and draw another. Start a Quick round to play 10 cards with a score, or just keep drawing for fun.

    • Everyone plays: the whole table joins in.
    • Spotlight: one player at a time.
    • Needs a thing: a pen, paper, a gift or a spoon.

    Five types of 16 cards: Spill, Showtime, Guess, Team Up and Cheers. Cards are meant to be fun for everyone: skip anything that does not feel right, and never pressure anyone. Toasts work with any drink, alcohol-free is just fine.

    After the first time you open it, the app works without internet. No account, no tracking. The only thing saved is your "No props" switch, on this device. Player names are not stored.
